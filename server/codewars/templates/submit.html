How it works

  1. The worker builds a docker image: official client + your files + compile.sh.
  2. If the build succeeds the version becomes active and plays rated sandbox games (about one game per strategy every few minutes, Round1/Round2/Finals formats).
  3. Build logs and your strategy's stderr of every game are on your strategy page (only you see them).
  4. Start a custom game to test against anyone, including your own older versions. Custom games are not rated.
