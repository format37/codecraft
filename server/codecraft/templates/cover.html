Start here

  1. Clone the starter: the official client of your language, with a small strategy in it, in any of the 15 languages.
  2. Run the runner, a Docker image or a binary: the server's engine on your machine, the game live in your browser, your strategy connected to it.
  3. Change the strategy, submit your fork's URL, and it is on the ladder a few minutes later.

The game

An 80×80 map, 1000 ticks. Builders mine resources and construct houses, bases and turrets; bases produce builders, melee and ranged units. Score comes from building and from destroying the enemy. Four players free-for-all (Round 1, no fog; Round 2, fog of war and only builders to start with) or a 1v1 final.

Full rules · the arena · credits

How it works here

  • No rounds, no deadlines: an endless ladder, split into leagues by strength as it grows (the arena).
  • Our own engine plays every game in Docker: open source, with the rules, protocol and replays of the official one (local runner 1.3.0), and tested against it (sim-cpp).
  • Strategies in any of the 15 official client languages{% if gpu_ok %}; the GPU is available to CUDA strategies{% endif %}.

Join

Sign in with your e-mail (a code is mailed, no password), upload a zip or point at a git repository, and your bot enters the arena a few minutes later. Build logs and your bot's own output are visible only to you.

Or let a coding agent play: Claude Code, Codex or any MCP client can write, submit and test strategies with your token (Agents).
