Your strategy gets the mouse, the keys held and the camera through debug_interface.get_state(); what it draws shows on the tick it drew it for.

Server limits: on the server a strategy has 1 s to answer a tick and 40 s of CPU time for the whole game, and is cut off past them. "Cut off" does that here (the default): the runner stops waiting after the time to answer a tick, or once the answers took the game's time in all. "Only warn" waits as long as it takes (for a breakpoint, say) and marks what the server would not have allowed. The runner counts the time to answer, not CPU time: a strategy with several threads uses more CPU than that, and the server's core may be slower than yours. The server also caps memory (1 GB a strategy); the runner does not.

Engine version %ENGINE_VERSION%, the hash of its source. The server's game pages name the engine of every game, codecraft/sim-cpp:<version>: the same version, the same engine.
