The charts, a line per player: score, resource in store, population (dashes: its limit), builders, melee units, ranged units, and counted from the attacks, resource mined, damage dealt to the other players and taken from them (the health it took), units killed and lost (not buildings). The lines run to the newest tick, fainter past the tick shown.

The same viewer is the local runner's, which plays a game live with your strategy and its debug drawings. Download the replay to watch it there, codecraft-sim-cpp --replay replay.dat, and save the state of a tick to start a game from (Ctrl-E): make -C sim-cpp in format37/codecraft.

Your strategy gets the mouse, the keys held and the camera through debug_interface.get_state(); what it draws shows on the tick it drew it for.

Server limits: on the server a strategy has 1 s to answer a tick and 40 s of CPU time for the whole game, and is cut off past them. "Only warn" (the default) waits as long as it takes (for a breakpoint, say) and marks what the server would not have allowed. "Cut off" does as the server: the runner stops waiting after the time to answer a tick, or once the answers took the game's time in all. The runner counts the time to answer, not CPU time: a strategy with several threads uses more CPU than that, and the server's core may be slower than yours. The server also caps memory (1 GB a strategy); the runner does not.

Engine version %ENGINE_VERSION%, the hash of its source. The server's game pages name the engine of every game, codecraft/sim-cpp:<version>: the same version, the same engine.
